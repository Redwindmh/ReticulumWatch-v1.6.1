# M0.3.9 notes

M0.3.9 fixes the reboot-loop found after M0.3.8 successfully started the
microReticulum core.

## Root cause

The identity restore path constructed `RNS::Identity(RNS::Type::NONE)` and then
called `load_private_key()`. In microReticulum, `Type::NONE` is an empty/null
Identity handle; `load_private_key()` requires a real backing Identity object
and asserts otherwise.

M0.3.9 constructs a normal `RNS::Identity` before loading the persisted 64-byte
private key. The generated temporary key material is immediately replaced by
the stored key. No filesystem erase or identity regeneration is required.

## Acceptance test

1. Flash M0.3.9 without erasing the Reticulum FAT partition.
2. Confirm `Reticulum core started`.
3. Confirm the firmware reports `loaded persistent 64-byte identity` rather
   than rebooting.
4. Record the identity and destination hashes.
5. Reboot once without reflashing and verify both hashes are identical.

The SX1262 remains in standby and M0.3.9 performs no RF transmission.
